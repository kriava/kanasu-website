About Sunita

A small introduction to the writer behind Kanasu.

SA
Sunita Avadhani · Brisbane, Australia

A journal for journeys, memories and everyday wonder.

Welcome to Kanasu, a personal space for stories, travel memories and reflections. Sunita Avadhani is based in Brisbane, Queensland, Australia, with stories connected to Sirsi and Karnataka, India, and places across Australia. More about Sunita will be added here in her own words.

This website is designed to grow with Sunita's writing: travel notes, personal essays, photo albums and useful place stories, all gathered in one welcoming home.
